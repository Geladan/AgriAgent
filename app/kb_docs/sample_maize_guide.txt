Maize Production Guide for Ghana (Sample Knowledge Base)

Planting
- Plant maize at the start of the major rains (March-April) or minor rains (September).
- Recommended spacing: 75 cm between rows, 25 cm between plants (about 53,000 plants per hectare).
- Plant 2 seeds per hole at 3-5 cm depth, thin to 1 plant after 2 weeks.

Fertilizer
- Apply NPK 15-15-15 at a rate of 100 kg/ha at planting or 2 weeks after germination.
- Top-dress with urea (46% N) at 6 weeks after planting at 50 kg/ha.
- Apply fertilizer when the soil is moist, then cover with soil.

Common Pests
- Fall armyworm: check the whorl for larvae and frass. Control with emamectin benzoate or
  spinetoram early in the morning. Hand-pick visible larvae.
- Stem borer: apply granules into the whorl 3-4 weeks after planting.
- Aphids: use neem oil spray or systemic insecticide.

Common Diseases
- Maize streak virus: transmitted by leafhoppers; use resistant varieties and control leafhoppers.
- Grey leaf spot: remove infected leaves, improve airflow, rotate crops.
- Rust: apply fungicide at first sign; avoid overhead watering.

Harvest
- Maize is ready 90-120 days after planting depending on variety.
- Harvest when the husks are dry and the kernels are hard.
- Dry cobs for 2-3 weeks before shelling; store at moisture below 13% to prevent aflatoxin.

Market
- Sell at the local market or through aggregators; prices peak in the lean season (June-July).
- Store in hermetic bags (e.g. Purdue Improved Crop Storage) to reduce post-harvest losses.
